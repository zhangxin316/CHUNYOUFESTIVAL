CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2014

2014年的春游，继续从成都出发。

第二年的春游开始拥有了更加完整的现场结构， 音乐、朋友与城市中的各种相遇，继续在这个春天发生。

与第一年的聚会相比，春游开始逐渐形成自己的样貌。

更多的音乐人、艺术家和观众加入其中， 春游也开始从一次朋友之间的聚会， 慢慢变成一个属于更多人的音乐与文化活动。

In 2014, CHUNYOU continued its journey from Chengdu.

The second edition began to develop a more complete structure, bringing together music, friends and unexpected encounters throughout the city.

Compared with the first gathering, CHUNYOU gradually began to develop its own identity.

More musicians, artists and audiences became part of the gathering, and CHUNYOU slowly grew from a gathering among friends into a music and cultural event for a wider community.
