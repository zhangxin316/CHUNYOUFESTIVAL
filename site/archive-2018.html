CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2018

拆迁的魔咒似乎总是困扰着“早上好”。

东篱菊园也没能逃脱被拆迁的命运。

于是，2018年的“春游”离开了三圣乡， 搬到了东洪路的梵木创艺区。

到那个时候，“早上好”已经经历了9年被拆4家店的荒诞经历—— 小通巷、民主路、红砂村和东篱菊园。

实体店不断面对的不确定， 让我们开始重新思考“早上好”的未来。 最终，我们决定放下经营了9年的实体店身份， 开始转向一个完全独立的音乐主办方。

“春游”也跟着一起改变。

从2017年的1000人，到2018年的2000人。 它离开了最初生长的三圣乡， 也开始进入一个新的阶段。

The curse of demolition seemed to follow Morning wherever it went.

Dongli Juyuan was the next to fall.

As a result, in 2018 CHUNYOU left Sanshengxiang and moved to Fanmu Creative Park on Donghong Road.

By then, Morning had been through the absurd experience of losing four venues to demolition in nine years — Xiaotong Lane, Minzhu Road, Hongsha Village, and Dongli Juyuan.

The constant uncertainty surrounding a permanent venue forced us to rethink Morning’s future. Eventually, we decided to leave behind the identity of a physical venue that we had carried for nine years, and began the transition into a fully independent music organizer.

CHUNYOU changed with it.

From 1,000 people in 2017 to 2,000 in 2018. It left Sanshengxiang, where it had first taken root, and entered a new chapter.
