2017

2017年，春游继续在成都发生。

这一年，春游在不断变化的城市空间中继续寻找自己的位置， 音乐、艺术与朋友之间的连接也变得更加自然。

从最初的聚会，到逐渐形成属于自己的文化现场， 春游开始积累起更加清晰的气质与记忆。

这一年的经历，也成为后来春游不断发展和变化的重要一部分。

In 2017, CHUNYOU continued to take place in Chengdu.

During this year, CHUNYOU kept searching for its place within a city that was constantly changing. The connections between music, art and friends also became more natural and more deeply rooted.

What had begun as a gathering was gradually becoming a cultural space with its own atmosphere, identity and memories.

The experiences of 2017 became another important part of CHUNYOU's continuing growth and transformation.
