About CHUNYOU
关于春游

春游（CHUNYOU FESTIVAL）创立于2013年，是一个以独立音乐和青年文化为核心的文化节。

从最初的音乐现场出发，春游持续探索音乐、艺术与城市生活之间的连接，为年轻创作者提供表达、交流与成长的平台。

多年来，春游逐渐成为一个汇聚音乐人、艺术家与观众的文化现场，并持续推动独立文化的发展。

Founded in 2013, CHUNYOU FESTIVAL is a cultural festival centered on independent music and youth culture.

Starting from live music experiences, CHUNYOU continues to explore the relationship between music, art, and urban life, creating a platform for young creators to express, connect, and grow.

Over the years, CHUNYOU has become a cultural gathering place for musicians, artists, and audiences.

Organizer
主办方

MorningChengdu 于2009年在成都成立，致力于支持独立原创音乐，并为青年艺术家创造机会。

通过音乐项目和现场演出，MorningChengdu 持续推动年轻创作者交流，并与社区建立连接。

Founded in Chengdu in 2009, MorningChengdu supports independent original music and creates opportunities for young artists.

Through music projects and live performances, MorningChengdu encourages creativity and connection within communities.

Network
网络

自2013年以来，春游逐渐建立起一个连接音乐人、艺术家、空间、机构以及参与者的文化网络。

通过多年的合作与交流，这些关系成为春游持续发展的重要基础。

Since 2013, CHUNYOU has been building a cultural network that connects musicians, artists, creative spaces, organizations, and communities.

Through years of collaboration and exchange, these connections have become an essential part of CHUNYOU’s identity.
