CHUNYOU 2013

April 20, 2013
Morning Chengdu

成都的春天，雨水较少，阳光明媚，温度舒适。

“春游”这个词，承载着很多中国人童年时期难以磨灭的记忆。我们希望通过音乐演出与派对的方式，重新寻找那份属于童年的简单与快乐。

2013年4月20日，第一次“春游”在 Morning 成都实体店开启。

那时的春游还不能称为一个“节日”，它更像是一场朋友之间的聚会。两个简陋的舞台分别位于店铺的前院和后院，一个属于乐队，一个属于DJ；门口的公路上，则自然形成了一片二手市集。

参与演出的乐队和来到现场的观众，都是朋友、朋友的朋友，以及朋友的朋友的朋友。他们共同创造了一个自然、放松而愉悦的空间。

这份自由、开放，以及人与人之间的连接，成为后来每一届春游持续延续的情感基础。

Spring in Chengdu is usually mild and comfortable, with less rain, plenty of sunshine, and a pleasant temperature.

For many people in China, the word “Chunyou” (Spring Outing) carries unforgettable memories from childhood. Through live music and parties, we wanted to rediscover the simplicity and happiness of those early days.

On April 20, 2013, the first CHUNYOU took place at the physical space of Morning Chengdu.

At that time, CHUNYOU could not yet be called a “festival”. It was more like a gathering among friends. Two simple stages were built in the front yard and backyard of the space — one for bands and one for DJs. Outside the entrance, a small second-hand market naturally appeared along the street.

The musicians performing and the people who came to the event were all connected through friendships — friends, friends of friends, and friends of friends of friends. Together, they created a natural, relaxed and joyful atmosphere.

This spirit of freedom, openness, and human connection became the emotional foundation that continued throughout every CHUNYOU that followed.
