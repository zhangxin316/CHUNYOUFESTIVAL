2015

2015年的春游，继续在成都发生。

经过前两年的积累，春游逐渐形成了更加清晰的方向， 音乐、艺术与城市生活之间的关系也变得更加紧密。

春游不再只是一次春天里的聚会， 而开始成为一个持续发生的文化现场。

不同的音乐人、艺术家和朋友继续加入其中， 一起构成了属于春游的独特记忆。

In 2015, CHUNYOU continued to take place in Chengdu.

After two years of gathering and experimentation, CHUNYOU began to develop a clearer identity, bringing music, art and city life closer together.

CHUNYOU was no longer simply a gathering in spring, but gradually became an ongoing cultural space.

More musicians, artists and friends joined along the way, contributing to the unique memory and character of CHUNYOU.
