CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2013

成都的春天，雨水较少，阳光明媚，温度舒适。

“春游”这个词，承载着很多中国人童年时期难以磨灭的记忆。 我们希望通过音乐演出与派对的方式，重新寻找那份属于童年的简单与快乐。

2013年4月20日，第一次“春游”在 Morning 成都实体店开启。

那时的春游还不能称为一个“节日”，它更像是一场朋友之间的聚会。 两个简陋的舞台分别位于店铺的前院和后院，一个属于乐队，一个属于DJ； 门口的公路上，则自然形成了一片二手市集。

参与演出的乐队和来到现场的观众，都是朋友、朋友的朋友， 以及朋友的朋友的朋友。 他们共同创造了一个自然、放松而愉悦的空间。

这份自由、开放，以及人与人之间的连接， 成为后来每一届春游持续延续的情感基础。

Chengdu’s spring is known for its mild weather, bright sunshine and relatively little rainfall.

The word “CHUNYOU” carries deep childhood memories for many people in China. Through live music and parties, we hoped to rediscover the simplicity, curiosity and joy that belonged to those early years.

On April 20, 2013, the first “CHUNYOU” began at Morning Chengdu.

At that time, CHUNYOU was not yet a “festival” in the traditional sense. It was more like a gathering among friends. Two simple stages were set up in the front and back yards of Morning Chengdu — one for live bands and the other for DJs. Outside on the street, a small second-hand market naturally grew around the event.

The musicians performing and the people attending were friends, friends of friends, and friends of friends of friends. Together, they created a natural, relaxed and joyful space where people could meet and connect.

This spirit of freedom, openness and human connection became the emotional foundation that has carried CHUNYOU through every edition that followed.
