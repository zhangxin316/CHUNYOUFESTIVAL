CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2017

2017年，春游继续在成都发生。

这一年，春游在不断变化的城市空间中继续寻找自己的位置， 音乐、艺术与朋友之间的连接也变得更加自然。

从最初的聚会，到逐渐形成属于自己的文化现场， 春游开始积累起更加清晰的气质与记忆。

这一年的经历，也成为后来春游不断发展和变化的重要一部分。

In 2016, CHUNYOU entered an important new chapter.

The closure of “Zaoshanghao” in Sansheng Township, Hongsha Village, forced CHUNYOU to leave its original location and move to a new place nearby.

The change of location marked the beginning of a new stage. What had started as a relatively loose gathering was gradually becoming a more complete and independent scene. The era of CHUNYOU 2.0 had begun.

A new space brought new possibilities. Music, art, friends and the relationship between people and place began to come together in a freer way.
