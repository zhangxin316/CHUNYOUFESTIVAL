CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2019

2018年的春游接到了500多个噪音扰民的投诉电话。

这是一个完全没办法解决的问题。

2019年，在“丙火创意”的支持下，我们做出了一个艰难、 同时也非常具有挑战性的决定：

把春游从梵木创艺区搬到距离成都市区80公里、 车程一个半小时的黑龙滩梵木音乐公园。

因为对成都春天天气的盲目相信， 过于自信的我们终于出了一个超级大的问题。

第一次扩大规模， 就让我们结结实实地挨了一顿 “想法幼稚、欠缺思考”的毒打。

舞台被风吹走的那一刻，

只有两个字可以形容：

绝望。

但有时候，不得不承认——

错误的决定，也有可能抵达正确的地方。

自此，

春游开始了3.0时代。

In 2018, CHUNYOU received more than 500 noise complaints.

It was a problem with no real solution.

In 2019, with the support of Sunfire Creative, we made a difficult and highly challenging decision:

to move CHUNYOU from Fanmu Creative Park to Heilongtan Fanmu Music Park, 80 kilometers from downtown Chengdu and a 90-minute drive away.

Blinded by our faith in Chengdu’s spring weather, our overconfidence finally got us into serious trouble.

Our first attempt at expanding the festival became a brutal lesson in naivety and poor planning.

The moment the stage was blown away by the wind,

there was only one word for it:

Despair.

But sometimes, we have to admit—

even a wrong decision can lead you to the right place.

From that moment on,

CHUNYOU entered its 3.0 era.
