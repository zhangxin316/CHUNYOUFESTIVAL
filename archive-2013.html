CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2013

The first CHUNYOU FESTIVAL was born in Chengdu in 2013.

Starting from a small gathering of musicians, artists and friends, CHUNYOU created an open space where independent music, creativity and community could meet.

The first edition was held at Morning Chengdu, with live performances, DJs, a second-hand market and a free atmosphere created by the participants.

This beginning became the foundation of CHUNYOU FESTIVAL, continuing to connect musicians, artists, spaces and audiences through the years.
