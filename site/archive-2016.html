CHUNYOU ARCHIVE

2016

2016年，春游继续在成都发生。

这一年，春游在不断变化的城市空间中继续寻找自己的位置， 音乐、艺术与朋友之间的连接也变得更加自然。

从最初的聚会，到逐渐形成属于自己的文化现场， 春游开始积累起更加清晰的气质与记忆。

这一年的经历，也成为后来春游不断发展和变化的重要一部分。

In 2016, CHUNYOU continued to take place in Chengdu.

Throughout the year, CHUNYOU continued to find its place within the changing urban landscape, while the connections between music, art and friends became increasingly natural.

From its beginnings as a gathering, CHUNYOU gradually developed its own cultural atmosphere, character and memories.

The experiences of this year would become an important part of CHUNYOU's continued development and transformation.
